Rehearsal / pack rules

01Booking & payment

  • Pack must be paid before the first rehearsal.
  • Packs are for the registered band/group and are not transferable.
  • Rehearsal time must be booked in advance.

02Cancellation

  • Cancellation/rescheduling should be made at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Late cancellations count as a used session.
  • No-shows count as a used session.

03Time

  • Sessions start and finish at the booked time.
  • Arriving late does not extend the session.
  • If you finish early, the remaining time is not refundable.

04Extra time

  • Additional hours can be booked separately at the normal rate.

05Equipment

  • Users are responsible for the equipment during their session.
  • Do not move, disconnect, or modify equipment without asking the staff.
  • Any damage caused by misuse may be charged to the user/group.

06People

  • Packs include a maximum of 6 musicians/people.

07Food & drinks

  • Drinks are allowed only in designated areas.
  • No food or drinks near musical/electrical equipment.
  • No alcohol.

08Respect the space

  • Keep the rehearsal room clean.
  • Leave the room as you found it.
  • No smoking inside.
  • Respect staff, other artists, and neighboring sessions.

09Pack expiration

  • Packs expire after 3 months.
  • Unused sessions expire after that period unless Downstage approves an extension.

10Refunds

  • Packs are generally non-refundable once purchased.

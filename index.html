(03)OUR STORY

Downstage — A Tatmin Creative Space

MUSIC · COMEDY
PERFORMANCE · CREATION

Downstage is a creative and cultural space by Tatmin, dedicated to music, comedy, performance, and artistic creation.

Based in Rabat, Downstage brings together artists, musicians, comedians, producers, and audiences through live concerts, jam sessions, comedy shows, workshops, masterclasses, rehearsals, recording sessions, and artistic residencies.

As part of Tatmin, Downstage is more than a stage — it is a place to create, experiment, collaborate, and discover new talent.

From emerging artists to established performers, our goal is to build a space where creativity has a place to grow.
